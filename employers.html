💰 Hiring Deaf Talent Can Earn You Up to $2,400 in Tax Credits

Through the federal Work Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC), employers can claim up to $2,400 per Deaf employee hired through vocational rehabilitation — 40% of first-year wages. No cap on the number of hires. File IRS Form 8850 within 28 days of the hire date. (Tax advice: confirm eligibility with your accountant.)
